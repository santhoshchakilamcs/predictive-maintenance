# HPC Degradation (SAMPLE document written for this demo project. Not an OEM manual.)

## Symptoms
Gradual loss of compressor efficiency, rising exhaust gas temperature (EGT) at a given thrust, and slow drift in pressure-ratio sensors (sensors 11 and 13) over many cycles. Fuel flow creeps up to hold the same thrust.

## Likely causes
Fouling and erosion of high-pressure compressor blades, increased tip clearances, or variable stator vane scheduling drift.

## Recommended actions
1. Trend EGT margin and compressor efficiency over the last 50 cycles; confirm the drift is steady, not a single-sensor jump.
2. Schedule a compressor wash and borescope inspection at the next planned stop.
3. If EGT margin is below the fleet threshold, escalate to an engine overhaul assessment; otherwise keep the unit flying with a 25-cycle recheck.
