# Oil System Debris (SAMPLE document written for this demo project. Not an OEM manual.)

## Symptoms
Metallic debris in oil samples or chip detector alerts, sometimes with rising oil temperature or falling oil pressure. Debris quantity grows over successive samples.

## Likely causes
Bearing or gearbox distress, spalling, or abnormal wear in a lubricated component.

## Recommended actions
1. Quantify debris per sample and trend it; a rising trend matters more than one reading.
2. Run spectrometric oil analysis to identify the alloy and narrow the source module.
3. If debris is increasing or pressure is falling, ground the unit and inspect bearings; otherwise shorten the sampling interval and recheck.
