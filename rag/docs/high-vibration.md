# High Vibration (SAMPLE document written for this demo project. Not an OEM manual.)

## Symptoms
Vibration levels rising across cycles, especially at rotor-order frequencies, sometimes with a step change after an event. May come with noise complaints or visible rub indications.

## Likely causes
Rotor imbalance, bearing wear, blade rub, or a loose or damaged component after FOD.

## Recommended actions
1. Review the vibration spectrum: rotor-order peaks point to imbalance, broadband rise points to bearing distress.
2. Perform a borescope inspection of the suspect module.
3. If vibration is above limits or rising fast, ground the unit for balancing or bearing replacement; do not defer.
